# Uploading to YouTube

Everything is in this folder. Studio: https://studio.youtube.com, then **Create > Upload videos**.

## 1. Upload
- File: `vgi-explainer.mp4` (1080p30, 2:36, audio mastered to -14 LUFS)

## 2. Details
- **Title:** paste `title.txt`
- **Description:** paste `description.txt`. The `0:00 …` lines become chapters on the
  progress bar automatically; the three hashtags at the end show above the title.
- **Thumbnail:** upload `thumbnail.jpg` (1280×720). Custom thumbnails need a
  phone-verified channel: https://www.youtube.com/verify
- **Audience:** "No, it's not made for kids"
- **Show more > Tags:** paste `tags.txt`
- **Show more > Language:** English. **Caption certification:** "None"
- **Show more > Category:** Science & Technology
- **Show more > Altered content:** No. YouTube asks for disclosure when realistic
  content could be mistaken for a real person, place or event. A stock synthetic
  narrator over motion graphics isn't that, but ticking "Yes" does no harm if you
  would rather be explicit.
- **Show more > Allow embedding:** leave **on** (the site player needs it)

## 3. Subtitles
- **Video elements > Add subtitles > Upload file > With timing:** `captions-en.srt`

## 4. Visibility
- **Unlisted** first, to check the chapters and captions, then **Public**.

## 5. Send back
- The video URL (`https://youtu.be/...`). That's all the README thumbnail and the site
  player need.
